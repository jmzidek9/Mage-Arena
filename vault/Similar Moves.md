# Similar moves (reviewed 2026-10-27)

Every group below is moves that work or look alike. Nothing has been changed yet. Strongest overlaps come first.

## Very similar (near-duplicates)

1. **Two dragons circling the target.** Bone *Bone Dragon* and Ink *Ink Dragon*. Both ultimates circle the target for a few seconds and rain damage below.
2. **Hook that grabs the first enemy.** Blood *Blood Tether* and Soul *Soul Rend*. Both throw a hook into the first enemy and give you something back.
3. **Lob a glob that leaves a puddle.** Venom *Venom Spit* and Magma *Magma Glob*. Same basic attack, different colour (poison pool vs lava pool).
4. **Wide melee sweep in front of you.** Plasma *Thermal Lance*, Soul *Reap* and Ink *Brush Stroke*. All three sweep a 6.5 to 7.5 m arc, hit everything and shove it.
5. **Charge a piercing line shot.** Frost *Glacial Lance* and Steel *Railgun*. Hold, release, pierce a line, with a bonus at full charge. Laser *Ricochet Beam* is close too.
6. **Suck enemies in, then burst.** Sonic *Implosion* and Abyss *Maw of the Deep*. Gravity *Singularity* (ultimate) and Wind *Tornado* do the same thing bigger.
7. **Pin everything in an area.** Nature *Entangle*, Shadow *Shadow Snare* and Bone *Bone Cage*. All three hold every enemy in a circle in place.
8. **Launch enemies up from the ground at a target.** Earth *Stone Pillar* and Magma *Eruption*. Abyss *Grasp of the Deep* and Gravity *Levitate* also lift enemies, then drop them.
9. **Shield that blocks damage.** Crystal *Diamond Shell* and Bone *Bone Armor*. Both soak hits and then hurt enemies with the pieces.
10. **Rain projectiles on an area.** Cosmic *Meteor Shower* and Blades *Rain of Blades*. Both drop 12 things on the target area.
11. **Delayed blast on an enemy.** Fire *Fire Brand* and Explosion *Sticky Bomb*. Both stick to an enemy and blow up a moment later.
12. **Step-on trap that launches.** Rune *Glyph Trap* and Explosion *Proximity Mines*. Both are placed traps that blow when an enemy walks over them.
13. **Swarm of homing missiles.** Venom *Plague Swarm*, Soul *Soulstorm*, Arcane *Arcane Torrent* and Ink *Ink Koi*. All of them release things that hunt enemies.
14. **Beam that follows your aim.** Light *Solar Beam* and Plasma *Fusion Beam*. Light *Divine Judgment* is a pillar that follows your crosshair, which is very close too.
15. **Summon a creature that hunts the enemy nearest you.** Spirit *Spirit Wolf* and Arcane *Arcane Familiar*.

## Similar (same role, different twist)

16. **Homing basic attacks.** Light *Radiant Orbs*, Spirit *Soul Wisps*, Arcane *Arcane Missiles*, Illusion *Phantom Moths* and Blades *Flying Daggers*. Five basic attacks that all home in on enemies.
17. **Fan of three shots.** Nature *Thorn Volley* and Shadow *Shadow Daggers*.
18. **Piercing basic bolt.** Frost *Ice Shard*, Rune *Rune Bolt*, Bone *Bone Toss*, Wind *Wind Blades* and Sonic *Sound Wave*.
19. **Bolt that splashes on impact.** Fire *Fireball*, Plasma *Plasma Bolt*, Abyss *Void Bolt* and Ink *Ink Bolt*.
20. **Lobbed bomb.** Explosion *Grenade* and Crystal *Geode Bomb*.
21. **Bouncing or chaining hits.** Storm *Chain Lightning*, Bone *Cackling Skull* and Laser *Pulse Laser*.
22. **Turret that shoots the nearest enemy.** Storm *Tesla Coil*, Nature *Snaptrap*, Magma *Volcano*, Crystal *Spire* and Laser *Laser Drone*. Five versions of the same placed turret.
23. **Drag enemies toward you.** Water *Water Whip* and Steel *Magnetize*.
24. **Push everything away from you.** Gravity *Repulsor* and Plasma *Vent*. Wind *Gale Force* is the cone version.
25. **Thing that follows you around.** Storm *Thunderstorm*, Water *Rain Cloud*, Wind *Tempest* and Laser *Laser Drone*.
26. **Damage aura around yourself.** Magma *Molten Armor*, Wind *Tempest*, Light *Sanctuary*, Fire *Ring of Fire* and Abyss *Tentacle Ring*.
27. **Things orbiting you.** Cosmic *Orbiting Moons*, Bone *Bone Armor*, Steel *Blade Storm* and the Blades sword halo.
28. **Something huge falls on the target (ultimate).** Magma *Meteor*, Blades *Colossal Blade* and Fire *Phoenix Dive*. Storm *Thunderclap* is the small version.
29. **Repeating sweeps or pulses around a point (ultimate).** Soul *The Reaper*, Sonic *Bass Drop*, Earth *Tectonic Quake* and Laser *Prism Nova*.
30. **Disable one enemy for a few seconds.** Water *Bubble Prison*, Chrono *Stasis* and Arcane *Polymorph*. Crystal *Crystal Tomb* does it to an area.
31. **Blind or scare enemies in front of you.** Light *Blinding Flash*, Shadow *Blackout*, Spirit *Banshee* and Cosmic *Supernova*.
32. **Healing zone.** Nature *Lifebloom*, Water *Rain Cloud*, Light *Sanctuary*, Nature *World Tree* and Blood *Blood Moon*.
33. **Hold to pour, and it gets stronger the longer you hold.** Fire *Flamethrower* and Light *Solar Beam*.
34. **Launch yourself.** Wind *Updraft* and Explosion *Rocket Jump*. Earth *Stone Pillar* can also do it, and Blades *Blade Dance* is the dash version.
35. **Self buff for damage or speed.** Blood *Blood Pact*, Chrono *Accelerate*, Shadow *Veil* and Shadow *Eclipse*.
36. **Slowing ground.** Chrono *Slow Field*, Steel *Caltrops*, Ink *Pool*, Rune *Binding Circle* and Blades *Rain of Blades*.
37. **Damage spreads between enemies.** Sonic *Harmonic Link* and Venom *Contagion*.
38. **Line of ground damage ahead of you.** Earth *Fissure*, Venom *Serpent* and Explosion *Carpet Bomb*.

## Alike within one element

- **Rune:** *Glyph Trap*, *Sigilfall*, *Binding Circle* and *Word of Ruin* are all "place a ward, it fires when stepped on".
- **Abyss:** *Grasp of the Deep*, *Tentacle Ring* and *Kraken* are all tentacles grabbing or lashing.
- **Crystal:** *Prism Shard* and *Geode Bomb* both burst into crystal shards.
- **Chrono:** *Stasis* and *Time Stop* both store up damage and release it stronger.
- **Illusion:** *Mesmerize* and *Phantasmagoria* both charm monsters into fighting each other.
- **Spirit:** *Spirit Wolf* and *Army of the Dead* are both "summon spectral fighters".
