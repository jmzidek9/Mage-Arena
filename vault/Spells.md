# Spells

Replaced by [[Elements]]: the original three spells (Fire, Ice, Lightning) grew into 29 elements with 5 moves each (LMB, RMB, Q, E, R).

Movement: **Dash** (Shift, invulnerable, 1.2 s cooldown, shortened by **Blink**) and **Jump** (Space; Wind double jumps, and some moves let you glide while holding it).
