The big one: ultimates recharge themselves

Every hit adds ultimate charge (addUlt in js/sim.js damage()), and that includes hits from the ultimate itself. A big area ultimate refills its own meter almost at once. In 30 s, Storm cast Thunderstorm 25 times, Blood cast Blood Moon 16 times, Spirit cast Army 16 times against the boss, and Cosmic cast Supernova 14 times. Without its ultimate, Storm lands right at the median.

Fix once, in the shared code: damage dealt by an ultimate gives no ultimate charge (a noUlt flag that damage() checks). Then rerun the bench before tuning any numbers.

Effects that blind your own view

  • White-outs: Storm Thunderstorm, Cosmic Supernova, Spirit Army, Illusion Phantasmagoria, Fire Conflagration, Light Blinding Flash, Explosion Carpet Bomb.
  • Starts at your eyes: Light Solar Beam, Wind Gale Force and Tornado, Water Tsunami, Frost Glacial Lance and Blizzard cloud, Sonic Sound Wave, Illusion Mesmerize.
  • Things in your face: Steel Magnetize and Water Whip pull monsters too close, Spirit Wolf spawns on the camera, Cosmic Moons orbit at eye height, Venom wasps are too big.

The usual fix: start the effect 1.5–4 m in front of you, lower its opacity or flash, or cap the whiteFx value.

Caveats

  • Each move's damage is measured over 1.25 s, so fuses, summons and delayed blasts read low or 0. Use the 30 s kit and boss numbers.
  • The bot aims at the nearest enemy, so aimed area moves (Gravity's Crush and Singularity, Nature's Entangle) can land behind the pack.
  • Full per-element reports: playtest/reports/<element>.md. Raw data and screenshots: playtest/out/.
